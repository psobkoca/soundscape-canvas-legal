Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Soundscape Canvas ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices.

Information We Collect

Soundscape Canvas is designed with privacy in mind:

  • No Account Required: We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide personal identity details (e.g., name, email address) to use core app features.
  • Audio Processing: All spatial sound mixing, coordinate calculations, and procedural frequency generation occur entirely on your local device. We do not transmit or record your audio sessions.
  • Analytics & Purchases: In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple App Store and managed via RevenueCat. We collect anonymous transactional receipt data and device identifiers strictly necessary to validate entitlements and maintain active subscription access.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party provider:

  • RevenueCat: Handles in-app purchase validation and subscription lifecycle management. You can review their privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Data Retention and Security

Because we do not collect personal profiles or store audio data on remote servers, we do not retain or sell personal consumer records.
